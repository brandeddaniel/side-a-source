# Side A 0.5.11

- Two accounts at once: choose Open Terminal on an account, or type sidea use <name>, and that terminal stays on it while Autopilot moves everything else. Good for long tasks. sidea auto hands it back.
- Forecasts that know your week: "Runs out Fri 8 AM" and "On pace for 64%" now follow when you usually work, so quiet weekends and evenings count as quiet. A week-ahead line sums up all your Claude accounts.
- A cleaner menu: the Terminal setup prompt is gone. Pressing Use or turning on Autopilot sets it up.

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free.
