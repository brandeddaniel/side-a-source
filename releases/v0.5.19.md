# Side A 0.5.19

- Running sessions switch: when an account nears its limit, the Claude Code sessions already running on it continue on a fresh account within about 30 seconds. Nothing to quit or restart.
- Choose when to move: Settings > Autopilot > Move at (80% to 99%, default 95%). Lower leaves room for a burst; higher uses more of each account.
- Faster: Autopilot reacts as soon as a reply reports new limits, not once a minute.
- Safe: logins trade places, they are never copied, so no login is invalidated.

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free.
