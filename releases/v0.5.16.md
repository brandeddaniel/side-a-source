# Side A 0.5.16

- Numbers you can trust: signing in again, or Claude Code refreshing a login, makes Side A read that account right away. A reading older than 15 minutes shows the time it is from instead of passing as live.
- Safer logins: while Side A runs claude for an account, it holds Claude Code's own refresh lock, so it never renews a login at the same moment as a session you have open. Two renewals at once can get a login revoked.

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free.
