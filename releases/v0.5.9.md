# Side A 0.5.9

- Sync across your Macs: turn it on in Settings and Usage combines every Mac on your iCloud Drive, with a per-Mac breakdown. Accounts from your other Macs show up ready to sign in. Logins never sync.
- Follow Fable: while you use Fable, Autopilot picks the account with the most Fable left and skips Pro plans, where Fable is billed as extra usage. When every account has spent its Fable share, it tells you.

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel. Free.
