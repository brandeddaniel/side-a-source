Setup

  1. Drag Side A to Applications and open it. It lives in the menu bar.
  2. Add your Mac's login, then sign in to your other accounts.
  3. Leave Autopilot on.
Requirements

macOS 14+. Apple Silicon or Intel.
Python 3.9+ and Claude Code or Codex CLI.

Privacy

Your accounts and usage stay on your Mac. Claude and Codex handle sign-in and requests under their own policies.

Anonymous page, download-click, and demo statistics via PostHog. No cookies or recordings. Your browser’s privacy signals are respected. This page checks GitHub for downloads.
