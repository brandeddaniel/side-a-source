How Side A works

Claude Code and Codex plans come with a 5-hour and a weekly limit. Whatever you haven't used when a window resets is gone. With several accounts, one runs dry while another's quota quietly expires. Side A watches all of them and keeps you on the one about to lose the most.

What it does

Every limit, at a glance
5-hour and weekly usage for each account, with reset times. The menu bar shows the active account as a percentage.
Switches for you
New claude commands in any terminal use the account Side A picks. Running sessions are never interrupted.
Moves before the limit
It switches before an account runs out, and rechecks the moment a rate limit hits.
Starts windows early
Before your usual start, it opens idle 5-hour windows with one tiny message, so they reset sooner.
Shows where tokens go
Usage per day, project and model, read from your local transcripts.
Stays out of the way
It lives in the menu bar and runs in the background.

How it decides

It reads the same numbers as /usage, then scores every account:

priority= weekly % lefthours until it resets × plan size

Account A 60% left, resets in 24 h → 60 ÷ 24 = 2.5

Account B 90% left, resets in 144 h → 90 ÷ 144 = 0.6

A goes first. Its quota expires sooner.

Plan size
Max 20x counts 20, Max 5x counts 5, Pro counts 1.
Ties
Use the 5-hour window that resets soonest.
Limits
Leaves an account at 97%; only moves to one under 90%.
Calm
Stays put unless another account is 1.5 times more urgent.

What it touches

Reads
Each account's own login, where its CLI keeps it, and transcripts in ~/.claude/projects.
Writes
One marked line in ~/.zshrc if you turn on switching, and its own folder in Application Support.
Never
Copies, logs or uploads a login. Edits your Claude or Codex settings. Sends analytics unless you opt in.
